# Litepaper Tokenomics (Baseline)

## Purpose
This document provides a baseline, implementation-aware tokenomics framing for Ryvra prior to TGE.

## Program baseline
- Participation accrues **Proof of Transaction (PoT) points** under policy-defined rules.
- Initial PoT cap is **2 points/day/account**.
- Cap halving occurs proportionally by epoch according to governance-defined schedules.
- Q4 2026 TGE framing uses a **proportional allocation concept** based on finalized policy rules.

## Constraints
- Parameters not yet ratified are marked **TBD by governance/policy**.
- Distribution mechanics, eligibility, and exclusions are policy-controlled and versioned.

## Compliance disclaimer
PoT points are pre-TGE program units, not tokens, and convey no ownership or guaranteed future rights. All token-related design and conversion frameworks are subject to legal and regulatory review.
