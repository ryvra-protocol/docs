# Litepaper Tokenomics (Baseline)

## Purpose
This document defines the baseline, implementation-aware tokenomics framing for Ryvra prior to TGE.

It is intended as a policy-referenced working specification for participation accounting and distribution framing, not as a finalized issuance commitment.

## Program baseline
- Participation accrues **Proof of Transaction (PoT) points** under policy-defined and versioned rules.
- Initial PoT accrual cap is **2 points/day/account**.
- Cap halving progresses by epoch according to governance-ratified schedules.
- Q4 2026 TGE framing uses a **proportional allocation model** based on finalized policy rules and eligibility criteria.

## Policy and governance constraints
- Parameters not yet ratified are marked **TBD by governance/policy**.
- Eligibility, exclusions, anti-abuse treatment, and distribution mechanics are policy-controlled and explicitly versioned.
- Where implementation behavior and this document differ, governance-approved policy artifacts and canonical specifications are authoritative.

## Integrity and abuse-resistance considerations
- PoT accounting is expected to incorporate anti-sybil and anti-wash-trading controls.
- Suspicious or policy-noncompliant activity may be excluded, capped, or reclassified under published policy rules.
- Material rule changes must be traceable through changelog/version references.

## Compliance disclaimer
PoT points are pre-TGE program units, not tokens, and do not represent ownership, equity, debt, profit share, or guaranteed future rights.

Any token design, conversion logic, eligibility framework, or distribution pathway is subject to legal, regulatory, and governance review before activation.
