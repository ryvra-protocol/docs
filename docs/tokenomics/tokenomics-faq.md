# Tokenomics FAQ

## Are PoT points tokens?
No. PoT points are pre-TGE program units, not tokens.

## Do points guarantee future allocation?
No. Any conversion/allocation framing is policy-defined, governance-approved, and subject to legal/regulatory review.

## What is the initial daily cap?
The baseline cap is 2 points/day/account.

## Does the cap change over time?
Yes. Caps halve proportionally by epoch.

## Who can change these parameters?
Governance through the policy and change-control process.
