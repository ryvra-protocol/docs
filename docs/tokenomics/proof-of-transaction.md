# Proof of Transaction (PoT)

## Definition
PoT is a pre-TGE points program that quantifies qualifying activity under governance policy.

## Baseline mechanics
- Initial issuance cap: **2 points/day/account**.
- Epoch progression introduces proportional cap-halving.
- Qualification rules, anti-abuse controls, and exclusions are **TBD by governance/policy**.

## TGE framing
For a target Q4 2026 TGE window, PoT may inform proportional allocation modeling subject to final governance, legal, and regulatory approval.

## Disclaimer
PoT points are pre-TGE program units and are not tokens. PoT balances do not guarantee allocation outcomes and remain subject to policy revision.
